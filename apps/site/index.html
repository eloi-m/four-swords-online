On a touch screen: a stick under your left thumb, Sword, Item and Action under your right, and a pause button.

Hold the sword to charge a spin; Action lifts, throws and rolls. These are the defaults: change them in Shadow Battle's menu (Controls). Online, each browser plays keyboard 1, its first gamepad or the touch controls.

About

Shadow Battle, the 2–4 player arena mode of The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures (GameCube, 2004), rebuilt for the browser: on one keyboard with gamepads, against the computer, or online with rollback netcode.
